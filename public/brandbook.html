01 · Brand Essence & Coverage

Positioning, Corridors & Afghan Hubs

Strategic Architecture
Mission

One Connected Record

Customers do not need separate providers for export, customs handoff, and destination transit. JACXI connects USA & Canada pickup, Mersin or UAE routing, and Afghanistan release in one live record.

Visual Philosophy

Calm Enterprise + Refined Gold

Crisp white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) over a quiet soft gray shell (`#F9FAFB`), disciplined gold (`#D4AF37`) for primary action and emphasis only, and unmistakable semantic status states.

Core Capabilities

4 End-to-End Pillars

(1) Vehicle sourcing & auction transport (Copart / IAAI), (2) Ocean freight & RORO, (3) Export & import customs clearance, and (4) Secure port staging & consolidation storage.

Stage 1 · Origin Intake
USA / Canada Export Preparation

Auction acquisition, domestic towing, yard photo intake, CA/WA title verification, and port container loading.

Stage 2 · Dual Corridor
Mersin, Turkey or UAE Coordination

Dedicated container ocean sailing with live AIS/carrier tracking and route-specific port clearance & onward dispatch.

Stage 3 · Release
Afghanistan Transit & Delivery

Islam Qala overland transit, Herat Customs Department clearance, and provincial release across Afghanistan.

JxThemeSync Guard
Gold Override via MutationObserver

Because JXUI defaults to indigo (`#4f46e5`), `JxThemeSync.tsx` re-asserts `--jx-accent: #D4AF37`, `--jx-accent-halo: rgba(212,175,55,0.2)`, and `radiusScale: 'md'` (`8px`/`12px`).

Density & Button Sizes
Comfortable vs Compact Spec

Comfortable: `sm 32px / md 40px / lg 48px` (`8px` radius). Compact (`data-density="compact"`): `sm 26px / md 30px / lg 36px` (`6px` radius), root `14px`.
